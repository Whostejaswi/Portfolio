AutiPath

in progress

An adaptive indoor navigation system for autistic children, built around BLE + QR positioning, sensory-aware routing, and guidance that fades as confidence grows.

Kumaraguru College of Technology · Team: Tejaswi, Sanadhani, Preethi G (mentor)

Problem statement

Autistic children struggle to navigate unfamiliar indoor spaces — existing tools are either outdoor-only (GPS), built for adults, or purely physical with no digital positioning.

Solution

A smartphone app that combines BLE + QR indoor positioning with sensory-aware routing and AR-style guidance that gradually fades as a child's confidence on a route grows.

Approach

BLE beacons + QR re-sync for accurate positioning, Dijkstra's routing extended with sensory "cost" zones, and a confidence-score algorithm that scales guidance up or down automatically.

Status

Routing engine and four core app screens are complete. BLE and QR hardware modules are in progress ahead of the planned pilot study.
