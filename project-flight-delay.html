Flight Delay & Cancellation Analysis

completed

A full analytics pipeline over 200,000+ U.S. domestic flight records, surfacing the real drivers behind delays.

Personal data analytics project

Problem statement

Flight delays raise operational costs, waste airport resources, and hurt passenger experience — and because delays propagate across connected flights, understanding what drives them matters a lot for scheduling and operations.

Solution

An analytics pipeline built on 200,000+ flight records, turning raw operational data into actionable insight through statistical analysis, feature engineering, and interactive visualisation — surfacing delay causes, carrier performance, and high-risk travel windows.

Approach

Data cleaning (missing values, outliers), EDA to surface relationships between delays/airlines/airports/timing, feature engineering (Robust Scaling, One-Hot Encoding, log transforms), PCA down to 2 components retaining ~70% variance, and a Power BI dashboard for the final KPIs and trends.

Status

Completed — full pipeline and dashboard delivered.
