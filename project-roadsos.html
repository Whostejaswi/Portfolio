RoadSOS

completed

An offline-first emergency response platform giving instant access to nearby trauma-capable hospitals and emergency services.

Built for the IIT Madras CoERS Road Safety Hackathon

Problem statement

Road accidents often happen where victims or bystanders have no idea where the nearest emergency help is. Existing map apps focus on navigation, not on prioritising trauma-capable hospitals or consolidating ambulances, police, blood banks, pharmacies, towing, and puncture repair into one place — and connectivity is often unreliable exactly when it's needed most.

Solution

RoadSOS gives instant access to nearby trauma-capable hospitals, ambulances, police and fire stations, blood banks, pharmacies, towing, and puncture shops. It auto-detects the user's country for the right emergency numbers, and keeps working offline after the initial sync.

Approach

OpenStreetMap data is synced into a local MySQL database so searches run without internet. Hospitals are ranked with a capability-based scoring algorithm (infrastructure, specialties, operator type, bed availability) rather than distance alone, filtering out non-trauma facilities and estimating travel time with corrected road-distance calculations.

Status

Fully built and submitted for the IIT Madras CoERS Road Safety Hackathon, covering 200+ countries' emergency contact data.
